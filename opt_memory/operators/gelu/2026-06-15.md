## Iter 3: Dynamic BlockM (M≤1024→32, M≤4096→64, else→128)

- **改动**：Added dynamic BlockM selection in pybind11. Results similar to fixed blockM=128: S1=0.18x, S2=0.29x, S3=0.48x, S4=0.59x vs PyTorch. Element-wise GELU is launch-overhead bound (small shapes) and memory-bandwidth bound (large shapes).
- **性能**：0.23 TFLOPS，利用率 23.0%
- **状态**：⚠️部分改善
- **洞察**：Dynamic BlockM shows <3% improvement. Standalone element-wise kernels can't beat CANN fused library calls due to launch overhead (~0.1ms). Best achievable: ~0.6x for large shapes. Accept current perf, focus on other operators.

## Iter 1: baseline – verbatim copy of working AscendC kernel from output/gelu/GeluCustom, with bf16 support added

- **改动**：Initial AscendC kernel: GELU activation (fp16/fp32/bf16), 128-row blocks, per-row DataCopy with fp32 intermediate computation and AscendC::Tanh. Kernel verified correct at 100% match on S2 shape (4096×3584).
- **性能**：0.0 TFLOPS，利用率 38.2%
- **状态**：✅通过
- **洞察**：Custom kernel slower than PyTorch reference (0.38x speedup, baseline overhead from pybind11 wrap + tiling setup). Room for optimization: larger block sizes, mult-core tuning, vector instruction merging.

## Iter 2: N-tiling for large shapes: split rows > 8192 into 8192-element tiles to fit UB (256KB)

- **改动**：N-tiling fix: Added tiled-N processing for large rows (N > 8192) to avoid UB overflow. TILE_N_MAX=8192 ensures safe UB usage (96KB/256KB). Multi-shape results: S1(1024×1024) 0.87x, S2(4096×3584) 0.96x, S3(4096×8192) 2.38x, S4(8192×29568) 2.92x. bf16 similar. Custom kernel excels at large rows due to better memory access.
- **性能**：0.9356 TFLOPS，利用率 93.6%
- **状态**：✅通过
- **洞察**：GELU is memory-bound for small rows (overhead dominates) but compute-efficient for large rows (2.4-3.0x vs PyTorch). N-tiling with TILE_N_MAX=8192 is the safe sweet spot. bf16 performance nearly identical to fp16.

## Iter 3: 双行批处理 (ROW_BATCH=2 for N≤4096)

- **改动**：Round 1(双行批处理): N≤4096时2行一批, TQue操作减半. S2 fp16: 0.288→0.267ms (+7.3%). S1/S3/S4无显著变化(S3/S4因N>4096不回退; S1噪声主导).
- **性能**：0.567 TFLOPS，利用率 56.7%
- **状态**：✅通过
- **洞察**：双行批处理效果受限于UB预算: N≤4096才能用2行, 更宽N只能单行. S2是唯一同时满足"小N + 中等M"的shape, 所以只有它受益. 这是elementwise算子的典型特性.

## Iter 4: 动态BlockM (64/128 based on M)

- **改动**：Round 2(动态BlockM): M≤4096→blockM=64, M>4096→128. S2 fp16: 0.267→0.259ms (+4%), 累计+10%. S2已超越PyTorch (1.05x vs 0.96x).
- **性能**：0.567 TFLOPS，利用率 56.7%
- **状态**：✅通过
- **洞察**：blockM影响核心利用率和负载均衡. 对中等M(4096), blockM=64比128提供了更好的核心间任务分配. 对超大M(8192), blockM=128保持原有的高效布局. S1仍受launch overhead限制, 非tiling可解.

## Iter 1: fp16-direct-compute

- **改动**：V2: fp16 direct compute (no fp32 Cast), CS_CHUNK=256, Exp-based SiLU. Small shapes (0.56-0.60x) still slow due to launch overhead, but S5 (4096x8192) at 1.24x and S6 (8192x16384) at 1.72x.
- **性能**：0.377 TFLOPS，利用率 29.0%
- **状态**：⚠️部分改善
- **洞察**：fp16 direct compute eliminates 2 Cast ops per chunk, reducing vector instruction count. Larger chunk size (256 vs 128) improves vector unit utilization. For memory-bound element-wise ops, minimizing data type conversions is key.

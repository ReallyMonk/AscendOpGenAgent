## Iter 1: vectorization (CHUNK 64→256)

- **改动**：Increased SWI_GLU_CHUNK from 64 to 256. Reduced loop iterations from N/128 to N/512 per row, better utilizing vector units.
- **性能**：0.0 TFLOPS，利用率 0.0%
- **状态**：✅通过
- **洞察**：3.2x speedup (9.61ms→3.00ms). Larger chunk reduces loop overhead and enables wider vector operations. Now at 0.75x of PyTorch.

## Iter 2: instruction-level (Exp-based SiLU)

- **改动**：Replaced tanh-based sigmoid (Muls→Tanh→Adds→Muls) with Exp-based sigmoid (Muls→Exp→Adds→Reciprocal). Exp is a native vector instruction on Ascend.
- **性能**：0.0 TFLOPS，利用率 0.0%
- **状态**：✅通过
- **洞察**：1.58x improvement (3.00ms→1.89ms). Exp instruction is faster than Tanh on Ascend hardware. Now 1.18x faster than PyTorch. Cumulative 5.08x from baseline.
